# Recoil City engine audit

November 24, 2026 · Alwaysdata release `recoil-city-3d-radio`

| Area | Check | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perspective and input | Opened the city from the classic home in Chromium, walked, sprinted, jumped, rotated the camera, fired through the reticle, entered a vehicle, steered and braked. Switched views on foot and behind the wheel. | The character and car appear in the foreground in third person. First person moves to eye or driver height. A wall intersection shortens the chase camera in tight spaces. Touch controls and the software renderer also passed a playthrough. |
| NPCs and pursuit | Aimed at a moving pedestrian from third person. Took a moving cab using the vehicle interaction. Continued a pursuit up to five stars. | The pedestrian lost 19 HP and fled. The cab driver left and fled. Five stars produced three patrols at different street positions. Separate simulation checks exercised a defending pedestrian, blocked shots, reloads and wanted-level decay. |
| Vehicle damage | Steered the Vista into an obstacle at speed. Followed a damaged vehicle through a second impact, destruction, forced exit and replacement in the simulation. | The browser collision reduced condition to 79%. The simulation ejected the driver from the wreck and restored the abandoned car. Saved earnings survived. |
| Exploration and saves | Walked into the hidden Old Quarter radio alley, exited the city and reopened it. Exercised both new shops, three discoveries, jobs and garage resprays in the simulation. | The alley discovery survived reopening. Legacy city saves retain their cash, completed jobs, best time and Vista finish. Each discovery records once. The sandbox writes to its city save and preferences. Arena inventory, match settlement and account storage continue to use the existing game systems. |
| Rendering and population | Tested WebGL through Chromium's software GPU and the independent Canvas 2D perspective renderer with WebGL disabled. Sampled a stationary session in a 1000 × 690 viewport on Low. | Low produced a 692 × 477 framebuffer. One 3.5-second sample recorded 84 frame intervals, with a median of 32.2 ms and a 95th percentile of 80.8 ms. Low targets 30 fps and activates up to six nearby pedestrians. Standard and High allow twelve and eighteen. The crowd pool, replacement traffic and shot effects are bounded. |
| Audio | Decoded all 48 MP3s and compared every byte with the corresponding recording in the supplied fixed radio. Tested the main and City Settings players, track changes, repeat and original game cues in Chromium. | All 48 matched. The recordings total 25 minutes 46 seconds. The four existing synthesized cues remain selectable. The player streams a selected recording and supports HTTP byte ranges for seeking. |
| Offline and lifecycle | Installed the service worker, disabled the network, reloaded, entered from the classic menu on touch controls and started driving. | The offline shell contained 11 assets. The original City theme played offline. The other album recordings stream on demand. City exit disposes of graphics resources, releases input and returns focus to the home menu. |

The hash of the original “Welcome to Recoil City” MP3 is `c6cead51d65df5b9e4cb359dbb5f1ba063f1056185754f7e82d2186905438e0e`. The catalogue records a SHA-256 digest for each recording. [Soundtrack catalogue](../server/city-radio/tracks.json)

The 218-test suite covers the new sandbox checks and existing game systems. The frame sample is from a virtual Chromium machine. Physical phones, battery use and sustained frame rates across hardware remain unmeasured.
